# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR%20Analytics%20Dashboard.png)

## Overview
This project analyzes employee demographics and attrition trends using **Power BI Desktop**. Raw HR data was cleaned and transformed to visualize turnover patterns across departments, salary slabs, experience levels, and job satisfaction metrics.

## Key Metrics & Findings
* **Workforce Summary:** 1,417 total employees, 1,186 active employees, and 231 attrition cases (16.3% attrition rate).
* **Age & Experience:** Highest attrition occurs in the 26–35 age group and among staff with 0–10 years of experience.
* **Salary Tiers:** Turnover is concentrated in the 6–10 LPA and 0–3 LPA salary ranges.
* **Job Satisfaction:** Lower satisfaction scores (1–2) correlate with higher exit counts among Laboratory Technicians and Research Scientists.

## Technical Features & Visuals
* **Slicers:** Department and Age Group filters.
* **KPI Cards:** Total Employees, Active Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate %, Average Age, and Average Experience.
* **Charts & Tables:**
  * Donut Chart: Department-wise attrition.
  * Bar Charts: Attrition by salary slab and employee count by department.
  * Column Chart: Age group distribution.
  * Matrix: Job Role vs. Job Satisfaction levels (1–4).
  * Line Chart: Attrition trend over total years of experience.


## Tech Stack
* **BI Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **ETL:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** DAX measures for calculated counts and ratios
* **Data Source:** Excel HR Analytics dataset
